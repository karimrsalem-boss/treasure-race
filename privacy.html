Treasure Race - Privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026. Applies to the web version at karimrsalem-boss.github.io/treasure-race, published by DMK INTEGRATIONS - FZCO.

In short

This web version has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The game itself does not ask for your name, email or other personal details. It does rely on a hosting service and on multiplayer connection services, which process technical connection information such as IP addresses. These are described below.

Saved on your device

Your coins, rank, chosen sail, settings and any maps you make are saved in your own browser (local storage). The game does not upload them.

When you use the Map Maker, your photo is processed in your browser. The map picture made from it is saved on your device with the map. The game does not upload the photo.

Playing with friends

Multiplayer works between the players' devices. To set up a game, the following are sent over the internet:

  • Finding each other: the game connects to the PeerJS server 0.peerjs.com (peerjs.com). It receives a temporary game ID based on the 4-digit game code, connection setup messages, and your IP address.
  • Connecting: your browser may contact Google's STUN server (stun.l.google.com) and PeerJS's relay (TURN) servers. These see your IP address. If a direct link isn't possible, game traffic is relayed through a PeerJS server. WebRTC encrypts that traffic in transit.
  • Other players: the devices in your game receive game moves, your chosen sail and rank, and any custom map you play. As with any direct (peer-to-peer) connection, they may also learn your device's network address.

How long these providers keep connection data is controlled by them.

Hosting

The game is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep technical logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Game portals

If you play Treasure Race on a game portal (for example CrazyGames or GameDistribution), that portal runs its own software, shows its own ads and may use cookies or similar technology. The portal's privacy policy applies to that, and the portal handles any consent requests.

Children

Treasure Race does not intentionally ask players for personal information. Parents and guardians should also review the privacy practices of any game portal a child uses to play.

Contact

For questions, open an issue on the game's GitHub repository. Please do not post personal information in a public issue.
